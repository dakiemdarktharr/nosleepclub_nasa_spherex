## Decision

**Do not promote the correction or open the reserved data.** All **640/640** fits
complete and the separate numerical audit passes, but the absolute noiseless gate
fails: **70/128** clean cells satisfy all three requirements. **64** of those passes
use truth generated by the original fitting template; the independent Gaussian
family contributes only **6/64**. This is evidence of conditional template/phase
sensitivity, not evidence of improved sky accuracy.

The relative preservation guard passes **64/64 pairs**. It limits deterioration
from baseline to corrected within this test; it does **not** mean both models
recover the correct position or absolute flux. Eight of sixteen noisy clean
strata reach their observed-sample threshold. **252 old assessment cells and all
30 prospective fields remain unopened.** WISE 0855 measurements are not replaced.

## Noisy stability and missing companions

For each of sixteen clean strata, at least 15 of 16 planned draws must jointly
complete, pass numerical screening, have positive flux, radial error ≤0.5 native
pixel and flux ratio 0.5–1.5. All eight strata of geometry B reach 15–16/16.
All eight of A reach only 13–14/16. Thus **8/16 strata pass**, for both arms in
the same conditions; no robust accuracy improvement is established.

Each interval is two-sided exact 95% within its fixed stratum, without pooling
shared conditions as IID. Even 16/16 has a lower bound about 79.4%, and 15/16
about 69.8%; the sample threshold does not certify 90% population performance.
Mean flux Monte Carlo error in the archive is conditional on completed fits.

## Limits and the next acceptance decision

The sample has two selected old training geometries, one exposure, fixed
registration, diagonal observed noise, an imposed plane and one equal-brightness
companion geometry. It lacks source shot noise, uncertain neighbor coordinates,
measured independent PSF/SED truth, temporal variation, correlated noise,
calibrated covariance and blind detection. Gaussian independence is mathematical,
not instrumental. Matched truth favors the original renderer. No background
subtraction/EBL transfer function or sky completeness is established.

**Next milestone: freeze a development attribution experiment that separates
solid-angle convention, template mismatch and companion-model signal loss.**
Use only already opened geometries. Include symmetric matched controls for both
fitter families, and independent pixel integrals with the same physical flux and
solid-angle convention. Record target/background/neighbor projection and rank,
not just the best position or its numerical-support flag.

Acceptance before proposing reserved-data access:

1. Independently verify native units, masked integrals, full-plane flux and
   centroid normalization over the frozen phases. Separate convention changes
   from template changes, preserve source hashes/seeds, and freeze budgets before
   new outcomes. Do not reinterpret this failed historical gate as a pass.
2. Under a **new protocol**, a proposed candidate must pass every one of its 64
   clean noiseless cases (≤0.1 native px, 90–110% flux, numerical support), the
   paired preservation budget and declared per-stratum noisy guards. Retain the
   baseline, missing-source stress and all failures. Plan enough independent
   information for any population accuracy/coverage claim; N=16 is not sufficient.
3. Seal the candidate/evaluator before a complete new calibration check, resolve
   the two competing-mode failures, and obtain independent physical/leave-star-out
   PSF and uncertainty evidence. Only then consider a separately preregistered
   assessment-opening decision. The synthetic guard alone cannot authorize it.

The public next-step link points to the unchanged failed calibration gate. The
20-item production goal remains open, including independent scientific release,
recovery/rollback, representative load/SLO and full accessibility.
